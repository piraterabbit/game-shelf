Crown Hunters

Grab crowns, carry them to your throne, and bank them before the clock runs out. Dash into a rival carrying a stack to knock their crowns loose. Crowns come in rarer types: gold is worth 3, ruby 5 and rainbow 8. Emeralds give a speed boost and sapphires pull crowns toward you.

Round over

    WASD or arrows to move, Space to dash. On touch, drag anywhere to steer and tap Dash. Heavier stacks slow you down, so bank often. Walk through long grass to find eggs, then open them in the menu to hatch pets that keep their perks. Crowns you bank are saved, and you can spend them on eggs in the menu shop. Press M to mute sound.
