The cauldron

Add as many ingredients as you like. The colour comes from what you add. Every ingredient has its own ability, and adding more of it makes that ability stronger. Hover an ingredient to read what it does.

Hollow Brew

You are the last potion maker in the hollow. Walk the island, gather twenty-four kinds of ingredients and mix your own potions in the cauldron.
