How to play

  • Each turn, deploy troops onto your tiles, then move or attack.
  • Select a tile with 2+ troops, then tap an adjacent tile. Edges of the cube are adjacent, so you can march around corners.
  • Enemy tiles fight with dice until one side is spent. Defenders win ties.
  • Troops that just arrived (striped) cannot move again this turn.
  • A tile with 3+ troops fields your realm's creature: griffins, mammoths, fire foxes, vine serpents, hags or trolls. Bigger armies bring bigger beasts, and each beast turns to face the nearest enemy. Each creature has its own power, listed under your castle.
  • Capture a castle and that realm falls to you. Last crown standing wins.
