Work at a Pizza Place

Pick a job by standing at one of the colored job signs on the front wall, just outside the door, or use the job buttons. Coworkers fill every job you leave empty, so the pizza keeps moving.

  • Cashier takes orders from customers at the counter and hands over the finished pizza.
  • Cook builds what the ticket says (dough, sauce, cheese, toppings), bakes it, and passes it to the box table.
  • Box Boy boxes pizzas at the box table and puts them on the shelf by the counter.
  • Delivery takes boxed phone orders from the shelf, loads the car and drives them to the house.
  • Supplier carries crates from the supply pile out front to any station that is running low. No stock, no pizza.
  • Customer walks up to the counter, orders a pizza with the toppings you pick, and waits for the staff to cook it. You pay with your own earnings.
  • Manager mops spills, does paperwork at the desk, and can pay a team bonus that speeds up every coworker.

WASD or arrows to move and drive. E or Space to use. On a phone use the pad and the button.
